The four laws (v2)

Doctrine-diversity, not seed-diversity. Same doctrine + different seeds = byte-identical elites. Real trade requires houses that believe different things.
The receiver judges everything. Sender receipts travel as CLAIMS; the receiver's forge is the only judge at the border.
Re-trades are mimics. A patch that came from a trade is barred from re-export as new blood (measured: 0/2 bounce).
Shared-doctrine trades bounce. If both houses breed the same family, there is nothing to exchange.
